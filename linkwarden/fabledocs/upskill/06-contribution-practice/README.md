# 06 — Contribution Practice

Reading builds understanding; contributing builds judgment. Ladder:

1. [01-good-first-tickets.md](01-good-first-tickets.md) — 15 junior tickets (tests, docs, small extractions).
2. [02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md](02-mid-level-feature-tickets.md) — 10 cross-layer tickets requiring a design note first.
3. [03-senior-build-projects.md](03-senior-build-projects.md) — 6 multi-week projects with full planning surface.
4. [04-refactor-and-design-katas.md](04-refactor-and-design-katas.md) — 8 senior katas with self-grading.

Rules: every ticket answers *why useful, why small blast radius, what pattern it follows, how tested, what would get it rejected*. Design notes before code on anything cross-layer. Every completed item feeds a STAR story ([08/06](../08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md)) — the "Interview story potential" line on each ticket tells you which.

Upstreaming etiquette: check the real project's issue tracker and open an issue before significant PRs; training tickets here are valuable even if they never leave your fork.
